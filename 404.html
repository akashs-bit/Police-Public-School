ACADEMIC CALENDAR


The academic year for the school is programmed from June to the following April with brief breaks between academic terms. The academic program is compartmentalized into 2 terms for pre primary, primary and secondary levels. The first term commences in June, and the second term in October.

The timings of the school will be between 9.30 AM to 3.30 PM with staggered breaks for snacks and lunch.


a) Kindergarten/Pre-primary 8.30 AM to 12.30 PM
b) Primary/Secondary 9.30 AM to 3.30 PM

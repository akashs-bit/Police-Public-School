SCHOOL PROFILE DETALIS

In the region of Hyderabad Karnataka there is very much scarcity of schools that provide quality education at affordable prices for police personnel and public. With a view of providing high quality education to the children of surrounding rural areas Police Public School was started on 15th june 2019. It owes first step towards needed standard primary education which plays the most important character in laying the foundation of eduction for children.

The children need to qualify themselves to get admission into the school by attending an admission test. 30% of the admission is opened to the public in order to establish an excellent relation with the public and have a fair combination of students from different backgrounds. The school dedicates itself to the welfare of the police children by providing an opportunity for them to grow in an excellent academic environment for which it solicits unstinted co-operation from one and all.

The school epitomizes the quest for excellence in primary to secondary education.

The school has been serving as a temple of learning for all-round development of students, in H-K region offering wide variety of activities.

The school has attained huge amount of respect in the surrounding area. It will get succeeded in near future in attaining the noble cause of access, equity, quality and growth in the primary and secondary education.


OUR ORGANISATION

Police Public School is run by the Karnataka State Reserve Police multipurpose society as a welfare measure to provide quality education to the children of police force preferably to the children of lower strata of the police force


OUR DOCTRINE

  • -> To meet the educational needs of the children by providing a common programme of quality education.
  • -> To promote national integration.
  • -> To develop a wholesome personality in students.
  • -> To nurture physical, mental, intellectual, and spiritual components of children coming from various backgrounds.
  • -> To infuse the spirit of healthy competition and bring out the best in children.
  • -> To produce world class citizens who uphold our national spirit and secular values.
  • -> To produce individuals who believe in Indian values and ethics and to build a strong nation.
  • -> To promote self-reliance and self-confidence among children.

OUR VISION

Through teamwork and collaboration we will build a sense of community that is sensitive and commited to developing the whole child

"Learners Today,Leaders Tomorrow "
